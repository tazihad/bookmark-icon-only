## About this extension
Bookmark Icon Only lets you display selected bookmarks in the toolbar using only their website icons (favicons) — no text labels. This helps you keep a tidy, minimalist look while still having quick access to your most-used sites.

✨ Features:

Right-click a bookmark and choose “Show icon only” to hide its label.

Right-click again and choose “Show full bookmark” to restore the title.

Firefox sync support.

Works seamlessly with Firefox’s native bookmarks and toolbar.

Stores everything locally — no data collection or telemetry.

Keep your Firefox toolbar simple, organized, and efficient with just the icons you need.
